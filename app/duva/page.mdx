# Duva

[Duva](https://duva.app) puts every Gmail account you have into one inbox. Work, personal, the side project, the old company address you still can't let go of. One place, read as one.

Switching between Gmail tabs to check four inboxes is a small tax you pay dozens of times a day. Duva removes it.

### What Makes It Different

- **One inbox, many identities.** Mail from all your accounts lands in a single list, and you reply from the right address without thinking about it.
- **Ask Duva.** AI search across every account. Ask for the thing you half-remember instead of guessing keywords.
- **Less noise.** Info and promo mail gets sorted out of the way, so the inbox shows what needs you.
- **The basics, done right.** Snooze, star, categories, and calendar, built for someone who lives in their inbox.
